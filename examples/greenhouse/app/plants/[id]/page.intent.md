The care page for one plant.

- The plant's name as the heading, species underneath, and a status badge (needs water / OK).
- Soil moisture as a meter, plus how often it should be watered and how many days since it last was.
- A primary "Mark as watered" button that records it and refreshes the page.
- Location and notes.
- A way back to the plant list.
